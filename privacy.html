Privacy Policy

This policy covers the personal-use Gmail notifier and its public information website.

Information used

The private notifier uses its owner's configured sender and recipient email addresses, Google OAuth credentials and tokens, operational alert text, delivery timestamps, and delivery status. Alert text concerns private paper-agent operations, such as a failed scheduled job or interrupted data collection.

Only the gmail.send permission is requested. The app does not read Gmail messages, contacts, inbox contents, or mailbox settings, and does not collect a Google password.

Purpose and sharing

This information is used only to authenticate the notifier, send operational notifications, and track whether a failure or recovery has already been reported.

Email content and addressing information are transmitted to Google's Gmail API for delivery. The private runtime is hosted on a DigitalOcean server. Information is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. No data received from Google APIs, authorization files, or private agent records are included in this public website or its repository. The maintainer's contact email is deliberately displayed for support.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Authorization files are stored on the owner's local computer and privately on the server. The server restricts access to the token file and private directory through filesystem permissions. Google API requests use HTTPS. No passwords, tokens, or API keys are collected through this website.

Local files may also be copied by the owner's configured device backup or cloud-sync services, such as OneDrive, if enabled. Those copies are controlled through the owner's settings for those services and may remain after local files are removed.

The app records operational alerts and email-delivery metadata in its private journal. These records remain until the owner deletes them. Journal backups have a rolling 30-day retention period. Gmail may retain sent emails under the owner's account settings; deleting an app record does not delete those emails.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can stop Gmail access through Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authenticated sends but does not automatically erase earlier emails, journal records, or backups.

For removal of stored app credentials or operational records, contact the maintainer at luis4645@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, tokens, or API keys in a deletion request.

Website visits

This static website has no app sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, or app-managed cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Its handling of hosting data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

Maintainer: luis4645-ai. Questions about this policy may be sent to luis4645@gmail.com. Changes to the app's Google data practices will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
